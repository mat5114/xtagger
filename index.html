    Frequently asked questions

    Everything operators usually ask before choosing XTAGGER, from getting started to running and caring for the equipment.

    Getting started

    Do I need an internet connection?

    No. XTAGGER PRO uses its own dedicated router to create a private wireless network. You can manage and play games without an internet connection.

    How many players can play at the same time?

    Up to 250 players simultaneously, from small activity groups to large-scale events.

    What do I need to start a game?

    XTAGGER Taggers and a Remote Controller. You can add a wireless router with blended Radio/Wi-Fi technology and a Controller Tablet for a better player and operator experience.

    How is the equipment charged?

    All Taggers include individual chargers. For the full setup (vests, taggers, helmets or hats), charging stations support up to 20 pieces at once.

    Can I buy a complete package?

    Yes. Ready-made packages for 12, 16, 20 and 24 players. Custom configurations can be discussed for your requirements.

    Does XTAGGER support parallel games?

    Yes. A single router supports up to 4 separate playing zones, so multiple games run at once, with up to 250 players in total.

    For operators

    Is XTAGGER worth the upgrade for my venue?

    Yes. It has low operating overhead, warranty coverage, simple operation and professional after-sales support, and it is built to grow with your business: scalable capacity, free software updates and flexible setups.

    What are the running costs?

    Low. There is no physical ammunition and no recurring consumable cost.

    Is there a warranty?

    Yes. Equipment is covered against manufacturing defects, under the terms of your contract.

    Is it hard to operate?

    No. Setup is simple, controls are intuitive and the system needs minimal staff.

    What after-sales support do you offer?

    Professional technical assistance and maintenance support when needed, from our tech support centres in Europe and the United Kingdom: tech@xtagger.eu and tech@xtagger.co.uk.

    Do software updates cost extra?

    No. Software updates are free.

    Equipment care

    In what conditions can the equipment be used?

    From −20 °C to +40 °C, with relative humidity of 40% to 60%. Outdoors, play in dry weather or for up to 30 minutes in light rain; afterwards, dry the equipment and store it in a well-ventilated area.

    What safety precautions should I take?

    Avoid areas with strong electromagnetic interference, keep equipment away from corrosive environments, and do not operate during thunderstorms or near high-voltage power lines.

    How do I maintain the equipment?

    Clean it weekly with a soft, damp cloth, inspect cables, chargers and buttons monthly, and keep 10–15 cm of space around the air vents.

    How should I store it?

    In a dry, well-ventilated, dust-free place, between 0 °C and 30 °C with humidity of 30% to 60%. Remove the batteries from the tagger if you store it for more than one month.
